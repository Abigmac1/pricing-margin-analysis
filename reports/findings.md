## Commercial interpretation

Review the largest dollar exceptions with sales and finance before changing prices. Validate contract terms, special-price authorizations, rebate treatment, and competitive context. A below-floor sale is a review flag, not proof of an error. Prioritize opportunities that can be retained and monitor gross profit dollars as well as margin percentage.

The full-floor scenario assumes every exception can be corrected. It is an upper-bound arithmetic scenario, not a forecast. Volume sensitivities are assumptions, not estimated elasticity.
